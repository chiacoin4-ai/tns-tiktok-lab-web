Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Contact: iacointns@gmail.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the intended information handling of TNS TikTok Lab ("the App"), an independently developed desktop workflow for creating and managing original videos. Its TikTok integration is in development. The public informational pages on this website do not provide TikTok sign-in or posting functions.

2. Information the App may process

When relevant features are implemented and you choose to use them, the App may process:

  • Content you select: locally stored video, audio, images, captions, titles and drafts.
  • Account information: a TikTok display name, account identifier and other account fields actually included in the permissions you authorize.
  • Authorization information: credentials or tokens returned by TikTok's official authorization flow, used only to perform the actions that you approve.
  • Operational information: local logs and upload status required to troubleshoot video workflows.

The App will not request your TikTok password. It will not access TikTok account data before authorization.

3. Purposes and user control

Information is used to prepare your selected creative materials, display any available connected account status and perform the publishing functions you explicitly authorize. The App is designed to keep creative source files on the user's own computer. Posting to TikTok, if enabled, necessarily transfers the selected video and accompanying metadata to TikTok.

4. Storage, retention and security

Local content and operational records remain on your device until you or the software remove them. Future implementation of TikTok authorization must protect access tokens and provide a way to disconnect the account and remove locally stored authorization data. Access tokens should never be placed in public repositories or logs. This website does not itself maintain a TikTok-account database.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. Data is sent to TikTok only when the user authorizes the relevant action through an available integration. Hosting this informational website through GitHub Pages may involve routine technical processing by GitHub; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. The App and website are not affiliated with TikTok.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to connect a TikTok account, which content to process and whether to authorize a publishing action. You can revoke third-party app access using your TikTok settings. For questions, access requests or deletion assistance relating to the App, email iacointns@gmail.com. Rights under applicable data-protection law remain unaffected.

7. Children

The App is intended for users permitted to access the relevant third-party services under their terms. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Updates

As the App is developed, this policy will be revised to reflect any actual changes to functionality, storage or data sharing. The date at the top will be updated when material revisions are made.

Current status: This site is informational. The planned TikTok API integration is not active on these pages. Review the policy again before launching new data-handling features.
